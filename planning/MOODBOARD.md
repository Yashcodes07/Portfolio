# Moodboard — Emily Carter Portfolio

## Feel
Opulent, editorial, confident. A visitor should feel like they've opened a
luxury fashion magazine's creative-director issue — not a generic template.

## Palette
- `--color-canvas: oklch(0.16 0.03 15)` — near-black wine/maroon, the site's
  base ground (a creative director's portfolio should feel like a gallery at
  night, not a white SaaS page).
- `--color-ink: oklch(0.95 0.02 60)` — warm cream text on the dark canvas.
- `--color-accent: oklch(0.72 0.11 55)` — muted antique gold, used sparingly
  for numerals, rules, and CTA outlines.
- A secondary paper tone `oklch(0.93 0.02 60)` (cream) is used only for the
  "What I Do" panel to break the page into a light interlude, mirroring the
  reference composition. Ink-on-canvas and canvas-on-paper both clear WCAG AA.

## Type
- Display: **Cabinet Grotesk** (already wired) swapped for a serif feel via
  **Fraunces** (Bunny) for the oversized "PORTFOLIO" wordmark and headings —
  editorial, high-contrast, fashion-magazine.
- Body: **Satoshi** (already wired) for supporting copy — clean, wide-tracked
  uppercase labels.

## References
- A fashion magazine masthead: one word, full-bleed, oversized serif type.
- 1990s perfume-ad photography: dramatic single-color lighting, negative space.
- A gallery wall label: thin gold rules, small tracked-out caps, numbered plates.

## Structure (named after the subject)
1. **Masthead hero** — full-bleed "PORTFOLIO" wordmark behind a portrait, name/role/tagline below
2. **What I Do** — light paper panel, four service plates with imagery
3. **Selected Work** — three brand campaign cards on dark ground
4. **By the Numbers** — stat band (projects, clients, years, awards)
5. **My Process** — four-step numbered walkthrough
6. **Let's Talk** — closing CTA with contact details and availability card

## Departures from default shape
- Hero is a **type-only masthead with the portrait layered underneath the
  wordmark**, not the default text-left/image-right split.
- "What I Do" is a **light paper panel breaking a dark page**, not a plain
  three-icon row on the same background as everything else — and each plate
  carries its own photographic texture instead of an icon.
